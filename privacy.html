Privacy

Your CV stays in your browser.

European CV Studio does not require an account, login, or backend database.

  • Local storage: Your CV content, portrait upload, theme choice, and layout settings are stored locally inside your browser’s localStorage under your device and origin.
  • No CV transmission: Nothing you type into the editor, ATS Health scorer, or Job Keyword Matcher is sent to our servers.
  • Cookies: No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set by this site.
  • Portability: You can export your CV as a JSON backup or clear browser storage at any time inside /app.
  • Static hosting & fonts: Static files are served via Vercel, and display/UI fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
